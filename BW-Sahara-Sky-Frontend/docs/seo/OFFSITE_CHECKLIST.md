# Owner / founder corroboration checklist

Prepared 2026-10-07. Proposed tasks; no profile creation, outreach, posting, account submission or third-party verification has been performed.

- [ ] Approve the exact hotel name, one-liner, contact email, phone, address and map pin. Resolve whether the Gmail address or a domain mailbox should be the public primary contact; current choice follows the supplied booking email.
- [ ] Confirm inventory/features/bed counts, dome availability, meals/activities, booking conditions and payment instructions. Publish only approved facts.
- [ ] Identify a real founder or hotel representative if a named byline is desired. Until then, keep the website-team organizational byline; do not invent a person or founding story.
- [ ] Align the supplied [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Sahara.Starry.Sky.Camp), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bw_sahara_sky_hotel/) and [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@bwsaharaskyhotel) profile descriptions with the approved identity. Confirm account ownership and avoid conflicting telephone/email/address details.
- [ ] Claim/verify the actual Google Business Profile and Bing Places listing, if available. Record the real listing URLs and verified map pin; no guessed URLs in schema.
- [ ] Review any real Booking.com, Tripadvisor or other travel listings. Add only verified official property URLs to the entity registry. Invite genuine guest reviews without fake reviews, undisclosed incentives or scripted rating requirements.
- [ ] Consider a genuine YouTube room/access/experience walkthrough with accurate captions and multilingual transcripts. Add the channel to sameAs only after confirming its real URL and ownership.
- [ ] LinkedIn company/representative profiles are optional if used by the hotel. SaaS directories such as Product Hunt/G2/Capterra are not assumed relevant to a hotel.
- [ ] Seek credible travel articles, local tourism references or relevant interviews with disclosed relationships. Independent reporting is corroboration; copied hotel press releases are not independent validation. Measure quality rather than promise a mention quota.
- [ ] Respond helpfully in real travel communities, linking only when useful and declaring affiliation. Do not mass-post or create vanity Wikipedia pages.
- [ ] Approve deployment, provide access to Google Search Console/Bing Webmaster Tools, submit sitemap and save inspection evidence.
- [ ] Run the first three unbranded discovery prompts in fresh AI/search sessions and enter observed evidence in the ledger; record separate branded support prompts for factual accuracy.
- [ ] Review discrepancies monthly and update the site/profile evidence together. New sameAs URLs enter `src/content/entity.mjs`, followed by rebuild and schema tests.

Do not send account passwords or private guest/card data in the ledger. Record public source URLs or access-controlled evidence links only.
